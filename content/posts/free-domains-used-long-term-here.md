HapDNS supports **free renewal before expiration**, and you can renew your domain starting **7 days before the expiration date**.

As long as you renew it on time, you can continue using the domain for the long term and potentially keep it active indefinitely.

> **Important:** "Free forever" does not mean "no maintenance required."
> Make sure to renew the domain before it expires.

It is highly recommended to set a renewal reminder immediately after registering your domain so that you do not accidentally lose it because you forgot to renew.
