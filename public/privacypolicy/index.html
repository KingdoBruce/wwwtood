Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2026

We value the privacy and security of every visitor to this website.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit or use Tood.win, how that information may be used, and the choices available to you regarding your privacy.

1. Information We May Collect

When you browse this website or interact with us, certain information may be collected.

Information You Provide Voluntarily

If you contact us through comments, forms, or email, you may choose to provide information such as:

  • Your name or nickname
  • Your email address
  • Messages, comments, or inquiry details
  • Any other information you choose to provide

Please do not submit sensitive information, such as government identification numbers, bank card details, account passwords, or similar confidential data through public comment areas.

Information Collected Automatically

To keep the website operating properly, understand how it is used, improve performance, and maintain security, our servers or third-party service providers may automatically collect certain technical information, including:

  • IP address
  • Browser and operating system
  • Device type and basic device information
  • Date and time of access
  • Referring source and pages visited
  • Time spent on pages and basic interaction data

This information is generally used for analytics, troubleshooting, performance monitoring, and security purposes. It is not normally used by us to directly identify you as an individual.

2. Cookies and Similar Technologies

This website may use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to:

  • Remember necessary website preferences
  • Measure website traffic and usage
  • Improve page performance and browsing experience
  • Measure advertising and promotional performance
  • Provide more relevant content or advertising where permitted and where consent has been obtained

You can usually view, delete, block, or restrict cookies through your browser settings.

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect some website features, advertising preferences, or the overall browsing experience.

3. Third-Party Advertising

To help support the operation of this website, Tood.win may use third-party advertising services, including Google AdSense.

When you visit a page that contains advertising, Google and its advertising technology partners may receive certain information necessary to deliver and measure advertisements, such as:

  • The URL of the page you are visiting
  • Your IP address
  • Browser and device information
  • Data relating to ad impressions, interactions, clicks, or performance measurement

Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to measure advertising performance.

Where required by applicable law, information will only be used for personalized advertising after appropriate consent has been obtained.

You may also be able to manage your advertising preferences through the privacy and advertising controls provided by Google.

4. Affiliate Links and Promotions

Some articles on this website may contain affiliate links, referral links, or promotional links to third-party products and services, including services such as Airalo, Spaceship, and Vultr.

If you register, purchase, or subscribe through one of these links, we may receive a referral commission.

In most cases, this commission is paid by the service provider and does not increase the price you pay.

We aim to recommend products and services based on actual experience, usefulness, or relevance to the content. However, pricing, availability, features, terms, and service quality are controlled by the relevant third-party provider.

5. How We Use Information

Information collected through this website may be used to:

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the website
  • Understand website traffic and content usage
  • Improve navigation, layout, and user experience
  • Respond to questions, suggestions, or collaboration inquiries
  • Prevent spam, abuse, fraud, and security threats
  • Measure advertising and promotional performance
  • Meet applicable legal or regulatory obligations
  • Protect the rights, safety, and legitimate interests of the website operator, users, and other relevant parties

We do not intend to use personal information for purposes that are materially unrelated to those described above.

6. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

However, certain information may be shared or disclosed in limited circumstances, including where:

  • You have provided clear consent or authorization
  • Third-party services are needed for hosting, analytics, advertising, security, or website functionality
  • Sharing is necessary to respond to a request you have submitted
  • Disclosure is required by law, regulation, court order, or a competent authority
  • Disclosure is reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, or security of the website, its users, or other parties

Third-party service providers may process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

7. Users in the EEA, United Kingdom, and Switzerland

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, your personal information may be processed in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

Where required, we may ask for your consent before using non-essential cookies or processing information for personalized advertising.

You may be able to review, change, or withdraw your consent at any time through the privacy or consent management tools available on the website.

Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

8. Privacy Rights for Residents of Certain U.S. States

If you live in California or another U.S. state with applicable consumer privacy laws, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information.

Depending on your location and the applicable law, these rights may include the ability to:

  • Request information about the personal data we collect or process
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal information
  • Request deletion of eligible personal information
  • Learn whether personal information is shared or used for targeted advertising
  • Limit certain uses or processing of personal information
  • Opt out of certain types of advertising-related data processing

The specific rights available to you and the conditions that apply may vary depending on your jurisdiction.

9. Third-Party Links

This website may contain links to third-party websites, products, platforms, or services.

Once you leave Tood.win and access a third-party website, your activity is governed by that third party’s own terms of service and privacy policy.

We do not control how third-party websites collect, use, share, or retain your information.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any external service before providing personal information or using its features.

10. Data Retention and Security

We may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for website operation, communication, legal compliance, fraud prevention, security, and other legitimate purposes.

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against:

  • Unauthorized access
  • Unlawful use or disclosure
  • Accidental loss or damage
  • Malicious attacks or unauthorized modification

However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

For this reason, we cannot guarantee that information will remain entirely free from security risks in all circumstances.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live and the laws that apply, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion of certain personal information
  • Restrict or object to certain types of processing
  • Withdraw consent previously given
  • Request a copy of eligible personal information

To submit a privacy-related request, please contact us at:

Email: admin@tood.win

To protect personal information, we may ask for reasonable verification of your identity or the authenticity of your request before taking action.

12. Children’s Privacy

This website is intended for a general internet audience and is not specifically directed at children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below the age at which parental consent is required under applicable law.

If you believe that a child has submitted personal information to this website without appropriate parental or guardian consent, please contact us. We will review the matter and take appropriate action where necessary.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to website features, third-party services, business practices, or legal requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page, and the Last updated date at the top of the page will be revised accordingly.

We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how information is handled.

14. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, our use of cookies, or how personal information is handled, please contact us by email:

Email: admin@tood.win

We take legitimate privacy questions and requests seriously and will review them within a reasonable scope.
