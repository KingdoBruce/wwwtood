We respect the privacy and personal information of every visitor.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you visit or use this website, how that information may be used, and how you can manage your privacy choices.

1. Information We May Collect

When you browse this website or interact with us, we may collect the following types of information.

Information You Provide Voluntarily

When you contact us through comments, forms, or email, you may provide information such as:

  • Your name or nickname
  • Your email address
  • Comments, messages, or inquiry details
  • Other information you choose to provide

Please do not submit sensitive information, such as passwords, payment details, identification numbers, or bank account information, through public comment sections.

Information Collected Automatically

To maintain website functionality, analyze traffic, improve user experience, and protect website security, our servers or third-party services may automatically collect:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and operating system
  • Device type and basic device information
  • Date and time of access
  • Referring source and pages visited
  • Time spent on pages and basic interaction data

This information is generally used for analytics, troubleshooting, and security purposes. It is not normally used to identify you personally.

2. Cookies and Similar Technologies

This website may use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to:

  • Store necessary website preferences
  • Analyze website traffic and usage
  • Improve loading speed and browsing experience
  • Measure advertising and promotional performance
  • Provide more relevant content or advertisements where permitted

You can view, delete, block, or restrict cookies through your browser settings.

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect some website features, advertising preferences, or your overall browsing experience.

3. Third-Party Advertising Services

To help support the operation of this website, we may use third-party advertising services, including Google AdSense.

When you visit a page containing advertisements, Google and its advertising technology partners may receive certain information, including:

  • The URL of the page you are visiting
  • Your IP address
  • Browser and device information
  • Data related to advertisement views, clicks, and performance

Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to measure advertising performance.

In regions where consent is legally required, information will only be used for personalized advertising after you have provided consent.

You may manage your personalized advertising preferences through the advertising settings provided by Google.

4. Affiliate Links and Promotional Disclosure

Some articles on this website may contain affiliate links, referral links, or promotional links to third-party services, including services such as Airalo, Spaceship, and Vultr.

When you register, subscribe, or make a purchase through one of these links, we may receive a referral commission.

This commission is generally paid by the service provider and does not increase the price you pay.

We aim to recommend products and services based on practical experience, usefulness, and relevance. However, pricing, availability, features, terms, and service quality are controlled by the respective third-party providers.

5. How We Use Information

The information collected through this website may be used to:

  • Provide, operate, maintain, and improve the website
  • Analyze website traffic and content performance
  • Improve website structure and browsing experience
  • Respond to inquiries, suggestions, and collaboration requests
  • Prevent spam, misuse, fraud, and security threats
  • Measure advertising and promotional performance
  • Comply with applicable laws and regulatory requirements
  • Protect the legal rights and interests of the website, its users, and other parties

We will not use personal information for purposes that are clearly unrelated to those described in this Privacy Policy.

6. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information.

However, certain information may be shared or disclosed in the following circumstances:

  • When you have provided clear consent
  • When third-party services are required for hosting, analytics, advertising, or security
  • When necessary to respond to a request you have submitted
  • When required by law, court order, or regulatory authority
  • When necessary to protect the rights, safety, or legal interests of the website, its users, or other parties

Third-party service providers may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms.

7. Users in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we process personal information in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

Before using non-essential cookies or processing information for personalized advertising, the website may request your consent.

You may review, change, or withdraw your choices at any time through the privacy or consent management tools available on the website.

Withdrawing consent will not affect processing that was lawfully carried out before the withdrawal.

8. Privacy Rights for Residents of Certain U.S. States

If you are a resident of California or another U.S. state with applicable consumer privacy laws, you may have certain legal rights, including the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect or process
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of eligible personal information
  • Understand whether information is shared or used for targeted advertising
  • Limit certain uses or processing of personal information
  • Opt out of certain targeted advertising activities

The specific rights available to you may vary depending on your state of residence and applicable law.

9. Third-Party Links

This website may contain links to external websites, products, or services.

Once you leave this website and visit a third-party website, your activity will be governed by that third party’s own terms of use and privacy policy.

We do not control how third-party websites collect, use, disclose, or retain information. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies before using their services.

10. Data Retention and Security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for website operations, communication, legal compliance, security, and other legitimate purposes.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect information against:

  • Unauthorized access
  • Improper use or disclosure
  • Accidental loss or damage
  • Malicious attacks or alteration

However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure. We therefore cannot guarantee the absolute security of information transmitted to or stored by the website.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on the laws that apply in your location, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion of certain personal information
  • Restrict or object to certain types of processing
  • Withdraw consent previously provided
  • Request a copy of eligible personal information

To submit a privacy-related request, please contact us at:

Email: admin@tood.win

To protect your information, we may need to verify your identity or confirm the authenticity of your request before responding.

12. Children’s Privacy

This website is intended for a general audience and is not specifically designed for children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below the minimum legal age required in their jurisdiction.

If you believe that a child has submitted personal information without the consent of a parent or legal guardian, please contact us. We will review the matter and take appropriate action where necessary.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in website features, third-party services, legal requirements, or data-processing practices.

Any revised version will be published on this page, and the “Last updated” date at the top of the page will be changed accordingly.

We recommend reviewing this page periodically to stay informed about the latest version of this Privacy Policy.

14. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, our use of cookies, or how personal information is handled, please contact us by email:

admin@tood.win

We will make reasonable efforts to review and respond to privacy-related questions and requests.
